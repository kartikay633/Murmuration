Offline mode — design review

RMAFMTKS Rohan, Ana, Mei and you · started Monday

We want editing to keep working when the connection doesn't. Someone on a train should be able to write for twenty minutes, reconnect, and lose nothing — including whatever their teammates changed in the meantime.

What we're proposing

Every client keeps a full replica of the page in IndexedDB. Edits apply locally first and queue as updates. When the socket comes back, the client sends its state vector and receives only the part it's missing — usually a few hundred bytes.

  • No locks, and no “someone else is editing this” banners.
  • Merges are deterministic: every replica ends up byte-identical, whatever order the updates arrive in.
  • Comments stay attached to the sentence they were left on.

Open questions

How long can a client stay offline before we force a full resync? Snapshots every 200 events keep catch-up cheap, but a replica that's been away for a month still has a lot to replay.
